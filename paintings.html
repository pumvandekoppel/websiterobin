Series

Webcam

Year

2019

Medium

Oil on linen
[please view the website in fullscreen or on your phone to read the accompanying texts]

Initially interested in addressing the notion of painting and observation of how a romantic landscape has changed. In these early works using webcams hinted to the idea of a digital age. The geographical location wasn't of significance to me, the aesthetic quality and mundanity of these unchanging scenes attracted me. The surface of these works are heavily layered and abstracted giving disconnection from the process of painting within the works, and disconnection from the landscape as a source.
